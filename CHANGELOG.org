* Tower
** [0.3.0] - 2026-09-22
*** Added
- Animated player movement and directional sprites.
- Player attacks.
- Spell casting.
- Camera movement and improved 2D rendering.
- A simple inventory system.
- Multiple locations, including Home and Town.
- Location transition indicators.
- Scalable text rendering and improved font support.
- Dynamic texture atlas generation.
- Support for game mods and mod-provided assets.
- An FPS display.
*** Changed
- Split the game into "Game" and "Engine" components.
- Improved the build system.
