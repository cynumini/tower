* Tower
** [0.4.0] - 2026-10-20
*** Added
- 3D world rendering with depth, billboards, and camera controls.
- NPC dialogue and quest-giving interactions.
- A quest system with objectives to kill 10 zombies.
- Quest markers.
- Gold rewards for completing the quest.
- A main menu with Play and Exit options.
- An inventory HUD and hotbar selection.
- Hoe tool for removing grass floors.
- Iron ore deposits that can be mined with a pickaxe.
- Pickaxe tool for mining iron ore.
- Furnace that convert iron ore into iron ingots
- 3D buildings that hide blocks above the floor when entered, revealing the interior.
- Door and window objects.
- Slope blocks and triangular world geometry.
- Collision detection for solid objects and map geometry.
- Mouse-based block selection and destruction.
*** Changed
- Redesigned map data structures and rendering pipeline.
- Refactored inventory management into a modular system.
- Unified player actions across combat, interaction, and dialogue.
- Introduced a scene system and moved gameplay logic into a dedicated game scene.
- Reorganized engine, game, and rendering code.
- Improved window resizing.
** [0.3.0] - 2026-09-22
*** Added
- Animated player movement and directional sprites.
- Player attacks.
- Spell casting.
- Camera movement and improved 2D rendering.
- A simple inventory system.
- Multiple locations, including Home and Town.
- Location transition indicators.
- Scalable text rendering and improved font support.
- Dynamic texture atlas generation.
- Support for game mods and mod-provided assets.
- An FPS display.
*** Changed
- Split the game into "Game" and "Engine" components.
- Improved the build system.
